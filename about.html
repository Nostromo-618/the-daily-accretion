Why "Accretion"?

In astrophysics, accretion refers to the process by which matter accumulates—like how black holes grow by pulling in surrounding material. Similarly, I believe that knowledge grows through the gradual accumulation of understanding, one article at a time.

Thanks for joining me on this journey through space and time!
